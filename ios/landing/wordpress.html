Focus Techniques

4 Modes. One App.

Every brain works differently. Choose the technique that matches your style, and switch between them as your energy changes throughout the day.

🍅

Pomodoro Free

The classic. 25 minutes of focused work followed by a 5-minute break. After 4 cycles, take a longer 15-minute break. Perfect for tasks that need structured momentum.

25m work → 5m break → repeat 4x → 15m long break

🌊

Flowtime Pro

Let your focus flow naturally. Work as long as you want, and Pepinillo suggests smart break durations based on how long you focused. No rigid timers interrupting your flow state.

Smart breaks: <25m → 5m, <50m → 8m, <90m → 10m, 90m+ → 15m

📦

Time Block Pro

Set a fixed duration (up to 8 hours) and commit. No breaks, no interruptions — just deep, uninterrupted focus. Optional midpoint check-in to ensure you're on track.

Custom duration (1min to 8h) • Optional midpoint alert

⏱️

Stopwatch Free

Simple count-up timer with no goals or constraints. Perfect for tracking time on casual work, meetings, or any activity where you just want to know how long it took.

Count up • No breaks • Stop whenever you want
