### Description (max 4000 characters)
```
Pepinillo is the deep work companion designed to help you focus, stay productive, and build lasting habits — all with a beautiful, native Apple experience.

Whether you're a student, developer, designer, writer, or anyone who needs to get into the zone, Pepinillo gives you the tools to own your time.

FOUR FOCUS MODES FOR EVERY STYLE

• Pomodoro — The classic 25/5 technique. Stay sharp with structured work and break intervals.
• Flowtime — Start working and Pepinillo suggests breaks when you need them. Perfect for creative flow.
• Time Block — Dedicate a set block of time to a single task with optional midpoint check-ins.
• Stopwatch — Freeform timing for when you just need to track how long you worked.

POWERFUL TASK MANAGEMENT

Create tasks, add subtasks, set priorities, and link them directly to your focus sessions. Import tasks from Apple Reminders so everything lives in one place. Tag tasks as Deep Work or Shallow Work to understand where your time really goes.

ANALYTICS THAT MATTER

See your daily, weekly, and yearly focus stats at a glance. Discover your peak productivity hours, best days, and how your time is distributed across modes. Track your streak and unlock achievements as you build consistency.

Weekly Review gives you a complete picture: total focus time, sessions completed, dominant mode, and detailed breakdowns — so you always know if you're improving.

SOUNDSCAPES TO STAY IN THE ZONE

Choose from 8 ambient soundscapes — Rain, Forest, Café, White Noise, Ocean, Fireplace, Binaural Beats, and Lo-fi — designed to help you concentrate and block distractions.

BEAUTIFULLY INTEGRATED WITH APPLE

• Live Activities & Dynamic Island — See your timer on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island without opening the app.
• Home Screen & Lock Screen Widgets — Glance at your daily progress, active timer, or streak.
• Apple Watch — Full focus session control on your wrist with complications for quick access.
• Siri Shortcuts — "Hey Siri, start a Pomodoro" — control your sessions hands-free.
• Apple Health — Log Mindful Minutes automatically.
• Control Center — Quick-start a session from iOS 18 Control Center.

DESIGNED FOR YOUR BRAIN

ADHD-friendly guided breaks suggest activities like stretching, walking, or breathing exercises. Session journaling lets you reflect on each work session. Four beautiful themes (Classic, Forest, Ocean, Sunset) let you make it yours.

FLEXIBLE PRO UPGRADE

Pepinillo is free with Pomodoro and Stopwatch modes included. Upgrade to Pro to unlock Flowtime, Time Block, premium soundscapes, and advanced analytics.

• Monthly, Annual, or Lifetime — choose what works for you.
• No ads. No tracking. Your data stays on your device.

Start focusing better today. Your best work is waiting.
```
