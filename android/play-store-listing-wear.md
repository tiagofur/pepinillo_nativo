#### Main Copy
```
Pepinillo Wear OS brings the power of deep work to your wrist. As the companion
to the Pepinillo phone app, it lets you start, control, and complete focus sessions
without reaching for your phone. Stay in the zone and check your progress from your
watch face.

WRIST CONTROL FOR DEEP WORK

Your Pepinillo focus timer lives on your wrist. Switch between Pomodoro, Flowtime,
Time Block, and Stopwatch modes with a tap. See real-time progress with the focus
timer tile and quick-glance complication on your watch face.

PERFECT PAIRING

• Focus Timer Tile — Large display with progress arc, timer countdown, current phase,
  and session counter. Color-coded by focus mode (red for Pomodoro, purple for
  Time Block, green for Stopwatch). Instantly see if you're in a work or break phase.

• Tasks Tile — View your top 4 tasks with energy levels and Pomodoro progress.
  Tap to see full task details synced from your phone.

• Watch Face Complication — Mini timer display on your watch face for always-visible
  progress tracking without opening a tile.

• Stats Screen — Daily, weekly, and yearly analytics. See your best focus modes,
  session streaks, and productivity trends.

BIDIRECTIONAL SYNC

Sessions started on your watch appear instantly on your phone. Tasks created on
your phone update in seconds. Themes and preferences sync automatically—customize
once on your phone and see it reflected on your watch.

FOUR FOCUS MODES IN YOUR HAND

• Pomodoro — Classic 25/5 structured intervals.
• Flowtime — Open-ended focus with smart break suggestions.
• Time Block — Dedicate a fixed block of time to deep work.
• Stopwatch — Freeform timing for when you just need to track.

SEAMLESS NOTIFICATIONS & HAPTICS

Get haptic feedback when your session ends. Persistent notifications keep you
in control—pause, resume, or stop your timer right from the notification.

BEAUTIFUL & RESPONSIVE

Material You dynamic colors match your phone's theme. Clean, intuitive interface
optimized for small screens. High-contrast mode for outdoor visibility.

WHAT YOU NEED

Pepinillo Wear OS requires the Pepinillo phone app (free, available on Google Play).
Your watch and phone stay in sync via Bluetooth and WiFi.

START DEEP WORK FROM YOUR WRIST

Forget pulling your phone out every 5 minutes. Pepinillo on Wear OS keeps your
focus tool within arm's reach—literally. Get back to the work that matters. Download
the companion app today and sync your first session.

PRO FEATURES (via phone app subscription):

DESIGNED FOR FOCUS

No distractions. No ads. Your focus time is your own. All data syncs privately
```

- **Bidirectional Sync:** Changes on phone appear instantly on watch (and vice versa)
- **4 Focus Modes:** Not just Pomodoro—choose the technique that works best
- **Material You:** Theme syncs from phone, dynamic colors
- **Haptic Feedback:** Tactile completion notifications
- **Companion Experience:** Works seamlessly with the phone app (no standalone install)
