## 1. Market Repositioning

Pepinillo stops competing as a "Pomodoro app" ($3.99 commodity tier) and instead positions itself as a **deep work companion** ($25-50/year tier), where margins are higher, users are more loyal, and the competitive set is surprisingly thin.

### Previous competitive set (Pomodoro)

Forest, Focus Keeper, Be Focused, Flora, Focus To-Do — **saturated, low price ceiling, commoditized.**

### New competitive set (Deep Work)

Structured, Session, Things 3 (partial), Centered, Endel, TickTick, Focus To-Do — **less saturated, higher willingness to pay, more room for differentiation.**

### The market gap

**No app offers all of this together:**

1. Multiple focus techniques (Pomodoro + Flowtime + Time Block + Free)
2. Integrated task management linked to focus sessions
3. Full ecosystem integration (Apple: Watch, Dynamic Island, Siri, Focus Modes | Android: Wear OS, Glance, Quick Settings, Assistant, Health Connect)
4. Analytics that compare effectiveness across techniques
5. Localization and LATAM-first pricing

Each competitor covers 1-3 of these points. Pepinillo covers all 5.

## 8. Risks by Platform

### 8.1 iOS Risks

| Risk                             | Severity  | Mitigation                                                                               |
| -------------------------------- | --------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Scope creep**                  | 🔴 High   | Strict P0/P1/P2 prioritization. Ship MVP first                                           |
| **Apple rejection (App Review)** | 🟡 Medium | Follow Human Interface Guidelines strictly. No borderline features                       |
| **CloudKit sync issues**         | 🟡 Medium | Offline-first design. Sync is an enhancement, not a dependency                           |
| **Solo developer**               | 🔴 High   | Realistic scope. Use SDD for planning. Do not overpromise                                |
| **Structured as competitor**     | 🟡 Medium | Differentiate with 4 modes + tasks. Do not compete 1:1 on design; compete on flexibility |

### 8.2 Risks Android

| Risk                         | Severity  | Mitigation                                                                                                          |
| ---------------------------- | --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **OEM battery optimization** | 🔴 High   | Xiaomi, Samsung, Huawei kill background services. Guide users to whitelist the app. Use a robust foreground service |
| **Fragmentation**            | 🟡 Medium | Glance only works well on API 26+. Some launchers do not support resizing. Test on Samsung Galaxy as baseline       |
| **Low Wear OS adoption**     | 🟡 Medium | Wear OS is ~15% of the smartwatch market. Lower ROI than watchOS. Keep Wear OS scope lean                           |
| **Play Store discovery**     | 🟡 Medium | More competition and worse discoverability than App Store. ASO (App Store Optimization) is critical                 |
| **Play Billing complexity**  | 🟡 Medium | Play Billing 7 + server-side verification. More complex than StoreKit 2                                             |
| **Pricing pressure**         | 🟡 Medium | Android users expect more free value. Use a less aggressive paywall than iOS. Generous free tier                    |

### 8.3 Cross-platform Risks

| Risk                             | Severity  | Mitigation                                                                                             |
| -------------------------------- | --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Feature parity**               | 🔴 High   | Keeping both platforms synchronized is difficult as a solo developer. Define a clear "parity contract" |
| **Cross-platform sync (future)** | 🟡 Medium | CloudKit (iOS) vs Go backend (Android) complicates sync across platforms. Solve in a later phase       |
| **Split resources**              | 🔴 High   | Two native codebases = double the work. Mitigate with SDD and strict planning                          |

## 9. Strategic Conclusions

1. **No competitor offers 4 focus modes + tasks + a complete ecosystem on BOTH platforms.** This is a genuinely open opportunity.
2. **Structured is the #1 threat on iOS** — it has Apple editorial relationships and strong design quality. But it only does time-blocking.
3. **Focus To-Do is the #1 threat on Android** — direct overlap in Pomodoro + tasks. But it has outdated UI and Pomodoro only.
4. **The $30-40/yr price point is validated** by Structured, Fantastical, Craft (iOS), and TickTick (Android).
5. **"Focus your way" / "Trabaja profundo. A tu manera."** is a powerful message — it positions us as the flexible option vs. single-technique competitors.
6. **LATAM remains an underserved market** — no premium focus app has a LATAM strategy.
7. **Users of Things 3 + Forest (iOS) and TickTick + Focus To-Do (Android) are our best acquisition target** — they already pay for tasks AND focus separately. Pepinillo replaces both.
8. **Coverage of 4 devices (iPhone, Watch, Android, Wear OS) is a unique differentiator** that no competitor can currently match.
